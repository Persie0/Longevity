# Gym program: longevity + modest muscle gain

Goal: maximize general health, maintain or modestly increase muscle, improve strength, and preserve time for cardio.

## Weekly structure

- **Mon — Full body A**
- **Tue — Moderate cardio 30–45 min**
- **Wed — Full body B**
- **Thu — Easy movement / optional cardio 30–45 min**
- **Fri — Full body A or B alternating weekly**
- **Sat — Optional vigorous cardio or sport**
- **Sun — Rest / walking**

This gives 3 strength sessions and ~2–3 cardio sessions/week.

## Full body A

1. Squat or leg press — **3 × 6–10**, 2 RIR, 2–3 min rest
2. Bench press or machine chest press — **3 × 6–10**, 1–2 RIR, 2–3 min
3. Chest-supported row — **3 × 8–12**, 1–2 RIR, 2 min
4. Romanian deadlift — **2 × 6–10**, 2 RIR, 2–3 min
5. Lateral raise — **2 × 12–20**, 1–2 RIR, 1–2 min
6. Calf raise — **2 × 8–15**, 1–2 RIR, 1–2 min
7. Cable crunch or ab wheel — **2 × 8–15**, 1–2 RIR

Approximate duration: **55–75 min**.

## Full body B

1. Bulgarian split squat — **3 × 8–12/leg**, 2 RIR, 2 min
2. Pull-up or lat pulldown — **3 × 6–12**, 1–2 RIR, 2–3 min
3. Dumbbell or machine overhead press — **2–3 × 6–12**, 1–2 RIR
4. Seated/lying leg curl — **2–3 × 8–15**, 1–2 RIR
5. Incline dumbbell press — **2 × 8–12**, 1–2 RIR
6. Overhead triceps extension + curl superset — **2 × 10–15 each**, 0–2 RIR
7. Farmer carry or Pallof press — **2–3 rounds**

Approximate duration: **55–75 min**.

## Progression

Use double progression.

Example for 3 × 8–12:

- Stay at the same weight until you can perform 12, 12, 12 with ~1–2 RIR.
- Increase load by the smallest practical amount.
- Re-enter the lower end of the range.

Do not add weight if technique or ROM deteriorates.

## Cardio

### Moderate days

Choose cycling, incline walking, elliptical, rowing, swimming, or easy running.

- **30–45 min**
- Talk-test intensity: can speak in sentences but breathing is clearly elevated
- Usually 2 sessions/week

### Optional vigorous day

Example:

- Warm-up 10 min
- 4 × 4 min hard
- 3 min easy between intervals
- Cool-down 5–10 min

Or use a sport you enjoy.

## Volume adjustment

Start at the listed volume. If progress and recovery are good for 4–6 weeks, add 1 set to lagging muscle groups rather than everything.

If fatigue accumulates:

- Remove 1 set per exercise, or
- train 3 RIR for a week, or
- deload for a week.

## Why this program is structured this way

It covers:

- squat
- hinge
- unilateral leg work
- horizontal press
- vertical press
- horizontal pull
- vertical pull
- calves
- arms
- core/carries

The three full-body days expose most muscles 2–3 times/week while leaving room for aerobic training.
