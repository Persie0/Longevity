# Minimalist and combined weekly plans

## Minimalist strength program: 2 days/week

For someone with limited time who still wants most of the health/strength benefit.

### Day A
1. Squat or leg press — **2 × 6–10**
2. Bench press or push-up — **2 × 6–12**
3. Row or pull-up/pulldown — **2 × 6–12**
4. Romanian deadlift — **2 × 6–10**
5. Optional lateral raise or core — **1–2 sets**

### Day B
1. Split squat — **2 × 8–12/leg**
2. Overhead press or pike push-up — **2 × 6–12**
3. Lat pulldown/chin-up — **2 × 6–12**
4. Leg curl or sliding hamstring curl — **2 × 8–15**
5. Optional curl + triceps superset — **1–2 sets each**

Effort: mostly **1–3 RIR**.
Rest: **2–3 min** compounds, **1–2 min** accessories.
Duration: roughly **30–45 min**.

## Ultra-minimal 20-minute circuit

Do 2–3 rounds, resting enough to keep technique good:

- Split squat — 8–15/leg
- Push-up — 8–20
- Pull-up/row — 5–15
- Hip hinge/RDL or single-leg hinge — 8–15
- Plank/side plank — 30–60 s

If no pulling setup exists, use prone Y/T/W and add a safe pulling setup when possible.

## Evidence-based combined weekly schedule

A practical balance for longevity + modest muscle gain:

### Monday
Full-body strength — **55–75 min**

### Tuesday
Moderate cardio — **35–45 min**

### Wednesday
Full-body strength — **55–75 min**

### Thursday
Easy walking / mobility — **30–60 min total movement**

### Friday
Full-body strength — **55–75 min**

### Saturday
Moderate cardio **30–45 min** OR optional vigorous intervals

### Sunday
Rest / easy walking

This normally produces:

- Strength: **3 sessions/week**
- Aerobic: **~70–130 min structured**, plus daily walking
- Optional vigorous work: **1 session/week**

To fully meet WHO's 150–300 min moderate-equivalent target, increase walking/brisk walking, add another 30–45 min aerobic session, or make some sessions longer.

## If muscle gain is higher priority

- Keep resistance training first in same-day sessions.
- Separate hard cardio and hard leg lifting by **6+ hours** when practical.
- Prefer mostly easy/moderate cardio.
- Add calories/protein sufficient for growth.

## If cardiovascular fitness is higher priority

- Use 3–5 aerobic sessions/week.
- Keep 2 full-body strength sessions/week.
- Include 1–2 vigorous sessions if experienced and recovered.

## If health/maintenance is the only goal

A very efficient floor is:

- Full-body resistance training **2×/week**.
- Moderate aerobic work **3×30–45 min/week**.
- High daily movement and frequent walking.

Doing less than this is still worthwhile; the biggest gain is going from no exercise to some consistent exercise.
