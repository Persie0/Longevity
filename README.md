# Longevity Nutrition

Evidence-based nutrition notes focused on **long-term health, cardiometabolic risk reduction, and modest muscle gain/maintenance**.

This repository is organized around practical decisions rather than single “superfoods”. The overall pattern is Mediterranean-style and plant-forward, with enough high-quality protein to support resistance training.

## Core idea

For a generally healthy adult, the best-supported pattern is:

1. Base most meals on vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, nuts/seeds and unsaturated fats.
2. Use extra-virgin olive oil as the main culinary fat.
3. Eat fish/seafood regularly, especially low-mercury oily fish.
4. Prefer legumes, soy, fish and fermented dairy as recurring protein sources.
5. Keep processed meat near zero and keep red meat modest.
6. For modest hypertrophy, aim around **1.6 g protein/kg/day**, with resistance training doing most of the actual muscle-building work.
7. Distribute protein across 3–4 meals rather than putting nearly all of it at dinner.
8. Aim for at least ~25–30 g fibre/day, preferably more when tolerated, using minimally processed plant foods.
9. Keep sodium, free sugar, trans fat and alcohol low.
10. Avoid treating fasting, supplements, individual “longevity foods”, or extreme macronutrient ratios as proven lifespan interventions.

## Important scope note

This is a general evidence synthesis, not individualized medical care. Kidney disease, pregnancy, eating disorders, diabetes medication, major gastrointestinal disease, food allergies, anticoagulants, severe hyperlipidaemia, or other clinical conditions can materially change the appropriate plan.
