# 7-day longevity + muscle meal plan

This is a template, not a calorie prescription. Scale grain/starch and protein portions to body size, appetite and training.

## Protein rule for every main meal

Try to hit **~0.3–0.4 g protein/kg per meal**. Add skyr/Greek yogurt, tofu, legumes, eggs, fish or a protein shake when a meal falls short.

---

## Monday

**Breakfast** — oats + plain skyr/Greek yogurt + mixed berries + walnuts + ground flaxseed

**Lunch** — lentil-quinoa salad with tomato, cucumber, peppers, leafy greens, herbs and extra-virgin olive oil

**Dinner** — salmon, barley or potatoes, broccoli/cauliflower, side salad

**Snack if needed** — fruit + kefir or yogurt

## Tuesday

**Breakfast** — eggs on whole-grain rye toast, spinach/tomato, piece of fruit

**Lunch** — tofu/edamame bowl with brown rice, cabbage, carrots, mushrooms and sesame

**Dinner** — chickpea and vegetable curry; serve with brown rice or whole-grain flatbread

**Snack** — apple + handful of almonds

## Wednesday

**Breakfast** — overnight oats with yogurt, chia, kiwi and berries

**Lunch** — sardines on whole-grain bread with tomato, rocket/arugula and lemon; vegetable soup or salad

**Dinner** — bean chili with mixed beans, peppers, tomatoes and corn; optional small amount of avocado; serve with whole grain

**Snack** — skyr/quark if protein is low

## Thursday

**Breakfast** — plain yogurt/kefir + unsweetened muesli + berries + pumpkin seeds

**Lunch** — lentil soup + whole-grain bread + large mixed salad with olive oil

**Dinner** — trout, quinoa, leafy greens and roasted vegetables

**Snack** — orange + walnuts

## Friday

**Breakfast** — tofu scramble with spinach, mushrooms and whole-grain toast; fruit

**Lunch** — chickpea/white-bean Mediterranean bowl with vegetables, olives, herbs and olive oil

**Dinner** — tempeh stir-fry with broccoli, bok choy, peppers and brown rice

**Snack** — yogurt or soy yogurt + berries

## Saturday

**Breakfast** — oats + skyr + banana + chia/flax + cinnamon

**Lunch** — hummus and roasted-vegetable whole-grain wrap; add edamame or lentil side salad for protein

**Dinner** — mussels or other seafood with tomato/garlic/herbs, whole-grain bread or potatoes, side vegetables

**Snack** — fruit + pistachios

## Sunday

**Breakfast** — Greek yogurt/skyr bowl with fruit, oats and mixed nuts

**Lunch** — whole-grain pasta with tomato, mushrooms, spinach and cannellini beans; olive oil

**Dinner** — choose one:

- vegetable frittata + lentil salad, or
- lean unprocessed chicken/turkey + roasted vegetables + barley, or
- tofu/tempeh if keeping the week fully plant-forward

**Snack** — fruit or kefir

---

## Weekly pattern created by this plan

- oily fish: 2 meals (salmon, trout)
- sardines: 1 meal
- shellfish: 1 meal
- legumes/soy: daily
- nuts/seeds: daily
- fermented dairy: most days if desired
- whole grains: daily
- vegetables: every lunch/dinner, often breakfast
- fruit: 2+ servings/day
- processed meat: zero
- red meat: zero by default; optional lean unprocessed serving can replace poultry occasionally

## Easy substitutions

- salmon ↔ sardines ↔ herring ↔ trout
- lentils ↔ chickpeas ↔ beans ↔ edamame
- tofu ↔ tempeh ↔ seitan + legumes
- skyr ↔ Greek yogurt ↔ quark ↔ high-protein soy yogurt
- barley ↔ oats ↔ brown rice ↔ quinoa ↔ buckwheat ↔ whole-grain pasta
- berries ↔ citrus ↔ apples/pears ↔ kiwi ↔ stone fruit
