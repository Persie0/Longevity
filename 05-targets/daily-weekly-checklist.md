# Daily and weekly targets

## Daily

- [ ] Protein: **~1.2–1.6 g/kg/day**; use ~1.6 g/kg/day for modest muscle gain
- [ ] Protein spread over 3–4 meals
- [ ] Vegetables: at least 3 servings
- [ ] Fruit: about 2 servings
- [ ] Legumes/soy: at least 1 serving on most days
- [ ] Whole grains/minimally processed starches: 1–3 servings depending on energy needs
- [ ] Nuts: ~20–30 g
- [ ] Seeds: ~1–2 tbsp, especially flax/chia
- [ ] Extra-virgin olive oil or another unsaturated fat as main added fat
- [ ] Fibre: at least ~25–30 g; ~30–40 g often practical if tolerated
- [ ] Water according to thirst, climate and activity; more with heat/exercise
- [ ] Free sugar low; sugary drinks ideally absent
- [ ] Processed meat: none
- [ ] Alcohol: ideally none or infrequent/small

## Weekly

- [ ] Fish/seafood: **2–3+ meals**
- [ ] Oily low-mercury fish: **1–2+ meals**
- [ ] Legumes/soy: **5–7+ days**
- [ ] Fermented food: several servings if tolerated
- [ ] Eggs: optional, moderate
- [ ] Poultry: optional, 0–3 meals
- [ ] Red meat: **0–2 meals**
- [ ] Processed meat: ideally **0**
- [ ] At least 20–30 different plant foods across the week is a useful diversity goal, though not a hard evidence-based threshold

## Best fish ranking for routine use

### Tier 1: best combination of omega-3 + low mercury

1. sardines
2. salmon
3. herring
4. Atlantic/Pacific mackerel (not king mackerel)
5. trout

### Tier 2: great lean protein, lower omega-3

- cod
- haddock
- pollock
- shrimp
- mussels/oysters/scallops

### Tier 3: useful but watch mercury/frequency

- canned light/skipjack tuna: better tuna choice
- albacore/white tuna: less often
- yellowfin tuna: less often

### Avoid as frequent choices

- bigeye tuna
- swordfish
- shark
- king mackerel
- orange roughy
- Gulf of Mexico tilefish
